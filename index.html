Our development policy

Security by design.
Care in every decision.

Our security-first policy puts protection into planning, development and review. We choose safeguards that fit your website, the information it handles and the services it connects to.

01

Secure foundations

Our policy prioritizes secure architecture, input validation and appropriate authentication and access controls. Private API keys and other secrets belong outside public frontend code.

02

Responsible data handling

We aim to collect only what the project needs and plan how that information is protected. Forms, integrations and access permissions are considered as part of the design.

03

Review before release

Our policy calls for security testing and deployment checklists appropriate to the project. Completed checks, known limitations and recommended fixes should be clearly documented.

Guided by established security references

Our development policy draws on applicable OWASP and NIST guidance. The controls and testing included in each project are agreed according to its scope.

OWASP Top 10 ↗ OWASP ASVS ↗ OWASP Cheat Sheets ↗ NIST SSDF ↗

These references describe our approach; they are not a certification or a claim of a completed independent audit. No website can be guaranteed 100% secure. Ongoing updates, monitoring and maintenance should be planned as part of the project.
